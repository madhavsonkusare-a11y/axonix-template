# Axonix

Read [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) first — it holds the rules, the map and the traps for this repo.

Two things that are never optional:

1. The owner's finance and identity data is never erased or modified unless they asked for that
   specific change. Follow [`.claude/skills/axonix-safe-change/SKILL.md`](.claude/skills/axonix-safe-change/SKILL.md).
2. Rebuild with `scripts/rebuild-app.ps1` after every change and verify the data survived.

For anything visual, [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md) records what the owner asked for and rejected.
