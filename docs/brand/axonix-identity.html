Nothing about the shape changes. The refinements fix how it renders small, where it sits in the icon, and how the name is spaced beside it. The mark also gets motion: it opens the app by pulling back from full screen, and its parts become the assistant's states.

The mark pulls back, and Home forms around it

The mark opens filling the window, centred on its body, then zooms out to its place beside the name. The greeting, the overview cards and the module tiles unfold from it. It is one scale animation plus fades, all on the compositor, so the app loading underneath can't make it stutter.

The mark

It already draws a neuron

Three round terminals feed one body that narrows to a single point. That reading drives the assistant's states and the loading pulse the app already has, and the startup zoom centres on the body.

The weight sits 10% low

The mark's box is centred at y = 124.6, but its ink is centred at y = 147.3. The heavy body is low and the top is a single small node. Centring the box leaves the icon looking dropped.

The refined icon centres at y = 136, halfway between the two. That is the usual optical compromise: the sharp point carries almost no weight, so its margin reads larger than it measures.

Wordmark

The name follows the mark

The old wordmark was heavy, tightly and unevenly spaced (the X and O overlap by 1.8 units), and set 6 units under the mark's point. The refined one is drawn from strokes on a 100-unit cap height, so every letter shares one weight.

Now
Refined
  • The A becomes a Λ. It drops its crossbar and takes the mark's point, sharpened with 4 units of overshoot so it stands as tall as the flat letters.
  • Spacing by shape. Gaps of 9 · 15 · 22 · 30 · 19 on a 100 cap: diagonals tuck in, the straight N–I pair gets the most air.
  • Lighter, so the mark leads. Stems are 15% of the cap height, down from 21%. Rounds run one unit heavier, N's diagonal 8% lighter, and X crosses above centre.

Vertical lockup: 38 units between point and name, up from 6. Horizontal: the name sits on the arms' axis (y = 170), so the right node leads into the A. Smallest: title-bar size.

The mark is the assistant

Four states, the same parts

The mark sits in the Home lockup and in a corner of every module, and it is how the assistant shows what it is doing. A state's loop runs only while that state holds; idle draws nothing, so an idle Axonix stays at zero restyles.

Thin glass: cards

Translucent fill, rim light and grain, with no per-frame blur. The ground behind a card is a still gradient, so blur would add GPU cost without changing how it looks.

Thick glass: things that float

Dock, command bar, sheets and menus. These pass over real content, so they blur and refract. The app's quick-liquid engine stays on sheets and selection lenses.
